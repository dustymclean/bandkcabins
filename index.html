About Us

The B & K Cabin can be found in Gloster, MS off a secondary road, nestled in the privacy of the Homochitto National Forest. Spanning amongst six acres of breathtaking beauty, you're sure to find privacy and tranquility you never knew existed.

We have been delighted to host hundreds of families & groups over the years.

It is rewarding to know that after their trip, guests find themselves anticipating & planning their next to return to the cabins comfort and beauty.

Why book your stay with us?

Generations of family gatherings, slow mornings and afternoons outdoors. Here is what we have always wanted our guests to know.

The B & K Cabin welcomes all guests year round, including all holidays.

And yes, by "all guests" this means your four legged family members, children of all ages, & your mother-in-law are welcomed to tag along & enjoy the fun!

Guests come through for simple getaways & family vacations quite often, both impromptu or meticulously planned.

Guests can do this due to the simple fact that we provide ample amenities & make booking your reservation easy, the way it should be.

In addition to typical visits, we have also served groups for weddings, yoga retreats, team building trips, family reunions, birthdays & anniversaries.

Contact Tina about current availability, guest limits, pets and gatherings before making plans.
