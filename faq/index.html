Frequently Asked Questions

A few useful things to know before your trip to the B & K.

What should I bring?

Don’t forget your coffee and creamer, shampoo, personal beach towels, and charcoal and lighter fluid if you plan to use the grill. Take a look at our full amenities page for the original detailed packing notes.

Are pets welcome?

Our original website welcomes four-legged family members! Please check current pet requirements with Tina before arrival.

Can we plan a gathering or event?

Our original site mentions weddings, yoga retreats, team-building trips, family reunions, birthdays, and anniversaries. Please contact Tina to discuss what is currently permitted.

How do we ask about availability?

Just call or text Tina at (601) 341-2663, or email bandkcabins@gmail.com.

Where is the cabin?

B & K Cabins is in Gloster, Mississippi, near Homochitto National Forest. Contact Tina for detailed arrival directions.
